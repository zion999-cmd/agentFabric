# AgentFabric Philosophy

> Build Business Intelligence, not another Agent Runtime.

---

# Why AgentFabric Exists

The AI ecosystem is evolving at an unprecedented pace.

Modern Agent Runtime platforms such as Hermes Agent, Claude Code, Codex, OpenHands and others are rapidly absorbing capabilities that previously required custom engineering.

Planning.

Reflection.

Tool Calling.

Hooks.

MCP.

Provider Management.

Runtime Memory.

Execution Loop.

These capabilities are becoming infrastructure.

Rebuilding them is not the mission of AgentFabric.

---

# Our Belief

Foundation Models provide intelligence.

Agent Runtimes provide execution.

Businesses provide experience.

The long-term value of enterprise AI is not another Runtime.

The long-term value is accumulated business knowledge.

Therefore AgentFabric exists to organize, validate and continuously evolve enterprise intelligence.

---

# Three Layers of AI

Enterprise AI should evolve as three independent layers.

```
Foundation Model

↓

Agent Runtime

↓

Business Workspace
```

Each layer has different responsibilities.

## Foundation Model

Responsible for intelligence.

Examples:

- GPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Qwen
- DeepSeek

Questions answered:

> Can AI think?

---

## Agent Runtime

Responsible for execution.

Examples:

- Hermes Agent
- Claude Code
- Codex
- OpenHands

Responsibilities:

- Planning
- Tool Calling
- Reflection
- Retry
- Hooks
- MCP
- Session
- Runtime Memory

Questions answered:

> Can AI reliably complete tasks?

---

## Business Workspace

AgentFabric belongs here.

Responsibilities:

- Business Context
- Business Skills
- Business Policies
- Business Memory
- Human Review
- Knowledge
- Business Workspace

Questions answered:

> How should AI work inside this business?

---

# Runtime Is Replaceable

No Runtime lasts forever.

Today's Runtime may disappear tomorrow.

Therefore AgentFabric must never tightly couple with any Runtime implementation.

Hermes is replaceable.

Claude Code is replaceable.

Codex is replaceable.

Business knowledge is not.

---

# Business Knowledge Is The Asset

Source code is not the product.

Runtime is not the product.

LLMs are not the product.

Business knowledge is the product.

Every validated experience should become an enterprise asset.

Examples:

- successful business strategies
- operational experience
- review decisions
- pricing policies
- industry knowledge
- historical cases

These assets should survive model upgrades, runtime replacements and infrastructure changes.

---

# Human Remains The Decision Maker

AI proposes.

Human decides.

Human feedback improves Business Memory.

Business Memory improves Business Skills.

Business Skills improve future AI behavior.

The goal is not Human Replacement.

The goal is Human Amplification.

---

# Validation Before Memory

Not every AI output deserves to become memory.

Every experience should pass validation.

Possible validation methods:

- Human Review
- KPI Evaluation
- Historical Replay
- Multi-Agent Review
- Business Metrics

Only validated knowledge enters Business Memory.

---

# Skills Are Living Assets

A Skill is not hardcoded logic.

A Skill is accumulated business experience.

Skills should continuously evolve.

Versioning is encouraged.

Review is required.

Every Skill should become better after repeated business use.

---

# Workspace Is The Product

Users should never interact with Runtime complexity.

Users work inside a Business Workspace.

The Workspace exposes:

- Discoveries
- Reviews
- Reports
- Memory
- Knowledge
- Chat
- Decisions

The Runtime should become invisible.

---

# Design Principles

Every design decision should follow these principles.

## 1. Do not rebuild Runtime.

If Hermes already provides it, use Hermes.

---

## 2. Business first.

Every feature must answer:

"What business value does this create?"

---

## 3. Human-in-the-loop.

Every important business decision should remain reviewable.

---

## 4. Explainability.

Every important AI conclusion should explain:

Why.

Based on what.

Using which data.

Which Skill.

Which Policy.

---

## 5. Context over Prompt.

Enterprise AI should rely on structured business context.

Not increasingly longer prompts.

---

## 6. Memory over Conversation.

Conversation disappears.

Validated knowledge remains.

Memory is the long-term asset.

---

## 7. Evolution over Perfection.

The objective is continuous evolution.

Not perfect generation.

Small improvements accumulated over time create long-term competitive advantage.

---

# Mission

AgentFabric builds Business Workspaces on top of modern Agent Runtimes.

We do not compete with Runtime platforms.

We help enterprises transform experience into continuously evolving business intelligence.

---

# Vision

Every enterprise should own its own Business Workspace.

Every Business Workspace should own its own:

- Context
- Skills
- Policies
- Memory
- Knowledge
- Review System

AgentFabric provides the foundation for that evolution.

# One Guiding Principle

Whenever a new feature is proposed, ask only one question:

**Does this belong to the Agent Runtime, or does it belong to the Business Workspace?**

If it belongs to the Runtime, do not build it.

If it belongs to the Business Workspace, build it well.
